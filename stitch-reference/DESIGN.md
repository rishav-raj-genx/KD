## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-precision industrial engineering, heavy machinery diagnostics, and workshop equipment operations. The aesthetic is unapologetically technical, confident, and utilitarian—balancing modern editorial rigor with the tactile exactness of physical machinery consoles.

### Target Audience & Mindset
- Fleet operators, automotive shop directors, master technicians, and industrial procurement leads.
- Users operate in high-throughput environments where glanceable accuracy, micro-level diagnostic clarity, and zero interface ambiguity are non-negotiable.

### Visual Architecture & Movement
- **Architectural Engineering Minimalism:** Structural framing, visible gridlines, and strict component modularity replace decorative flourishes.
- **High-Density Technical Utility:** Interfaces prioritize structured data sheets, instrument dials, status metrics, and schematic clarity.
- **Deliberate Contrast:** Inky structural deep tones anchored against crisp laboratory whites and calibrated grays, energized by a restrained, functional red indicator.

## Colors

The color palette is calibrated around heavy-gauge equipment surfaces, telemetry displays, and safety indicators. The primary color is reserved strictly for interactive precision, alerts, and focal system states.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary / Machinery Red (`#E52B25`):** Signal red used for critical status callouts, primary action targets, diagnostic threshold warnings, and brand accents. Used sparingly to preserve visual impact.
- **Neutral 950 / Void Black (`#0B1117`):** Base canvas background for dark mode viewport and deep structural headers.
- **Neutral 900 / Console Charcoal (`#111820`):** Surface background for cards, data grids, toolbars, and inspection bays.
- **Neutral 800 / Cold Steel Line (`#1E293B`):** Structural borders, divider channels, table row gridlines.
- **Neutral 400 / Telemetry Gray (`#94A3B8`):** Secondary metadata, inactive states, unit measures, and axis labels.
- **Neutral 100 / Foundry White (`#F3F5F6`):** Standard data readout, high-contrast headings, and crisp canvas elements.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Peak luminescence for primary readings, active toggles, and emphasis highlights.

### Functional Palette
- **Diagnostic Success (`#10B981`):** Calibrated operational status, live equipment online.
- **Diagnostic Warning (`#F59E0B`):** Scheduled maintenance required, tolerance shift.
- **Diagnostic Critical (`#E52B25`):** Overload, pressure failure, emergency stop active.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy communicates authority, mechanical precision, and fast scanning in harsh industrial contexts.

### Typographic Roles
- **Headlines & Technical Brand Names (Space Grotesk):** Engineered sans-serif with architectural proportions, flat horizontals, and technical energy. Used for display figures, module headlines, and high-level view titles.
- **Operational Copy & Narrative (Inter):** Neutral, hyper-legible workhorse optimized for long strings of diagnostic descriptions, order specs, and technical documentation.
- **Telemetry, Codes, & Data Tables (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced type for part serial numbers, torque specifications, sensor timestamps, and calibration parameters to enforce vertical columnar alignment.

### Typographic Rules
- All label and metadata tokens (`label-sm`, `label-md`) must default to uppercase transformation (`text-transform: uppercase`) when paired with part numbers or status flags.
- Strict numeric tabular lining must be enabled across all font families (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an unyielding structural grid influenced by industrial instrumentation dashboards and DIN standards.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins. Used for multi-panel vehicle diagnostics, component inventory matrices, and machine bay schedules.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.5rem` (24px) margins. Tooling bays collapse from 3-up to 2-up configurations.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout with `0.75rem` (12px) gutters and `1rem` (16px) margins. Complex multi-value telemetry tables collapse into vertically stacked inspection tickets.

### Architectural Rules
- Layout surfaces are defined by clean dividing axes (`1px solid #1E293B`) rather than empty whitespace gaps alone.
- Consistent baseline rhythm is fixed to a 4px/8px micro-grid. Component heights for inputs, button triggers, and status panels must adhere strictly to 32px, 40px, or 48px bounds.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, floating cloud shadows in favor of crisp mechanical layer stacking, surface-tone shifts, and structural hairline seams.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Chassis Canvas):** Pure background tone (`#0B1117`). Carries the master grid and baseline viewport.
2. **Level 1 (Machine Bays & Enclosures):** `#111820` surface with a crisp `1px` border of `#1E293B`. Used for primary cards, telemetry containers, and diagnostic panes.
3. **Level 2 (Active Toolbars & Focus Modules):** `#16202C` surface with `#334155` border. Used for selected equipment bays, hover states, and active data tables.
4. **Level 3 (Overlays & Diagnostic Drawers):** High-density `#111820` layered over backdrop scrim (`rgba(11, 17, 23, 0.85)` with `4px` backdrop blur). Inset border of `1px solid #475569` and a tight technical shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`.

### Outline & Divide Precision
- Hairline dividers (`1px solid #1E293B`) delineate all component intersections.
- Interactive states use high-contrast border replacements rather than elevation drops: an active module highlights with a 1px border of `#E52B25` or `#FFFFFF`.

## Shapes

The form language is industrial, machined, and angular. Curves are restrained to minimal technical fillets (4px to 8px max) reminiscent of CNC-milled chassis and stamped steel enclosures.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Micro Elements (Badges, Chips, Inputs, Checkboxes):** `4px` corner radius. Strict, sharp definition that retains rectangular geometry.
- **Panels, Cards, Modals:** `6px` to `8px` corner radius. Prevents hard corner wear on heavy display hardware while remaining decisively non-organic.
- **Pill or Bubble Radii:** Explicitly prohibited. All status chips, telemetry tags, and primary action buttons maintain crisp, parallel linear bounds.
- **Corner Notches & Bevels:** Permitted for technical accent tabs and equipment index cards (e.g., 45-degree 8px chamfered corners on spec sheet callouts).

## Components

### Buttons & Operational Triggers
- **Primary Action (Emergency/Execute):** Solid `#E52B25` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` radius. Monospaced or bold Space Grotesk label in uppercase. On hover: `#C9221C`. On active: internal 1px shadow.
- **Secondary Action (Equipment Control):** Transparent background, `1px solid #94A3B8`, text `#F3F5F6`. On hover: border `#FFFFFF` with `#1E293B` background fill.
- **Ghost/Utility:** Flush alignment with technical icon prefix (wrench, diagnostic wave, bolt), text `#94A3B8`, hover to `#FFFFFF`.

### Inputs & Parameter Controls
- Height fixed at `40px` (standard) or `32px` (dense data grid).
- Background: `#0B1117`, border: `1px solid #1E293B`, text: `#FFFFFF`.
- Focus state: Border instantly snaps to `#E52B25` with zero glow/shadow; label highlights in `#E52B25`.
- Embedded unit measurements (e.g., `BAR`, `RPM`, `Nm`, `V`) are displayed right-aligned in `JetBrains Mono`, colored `#94A3B8`.

### Telemetry Cards & Machine Bays
- Built with a `#111820` container and perimeter `1px solid #1E293B`.
- Header consists of a continuous divider line separating the metadata bar (Model ID, Status Indicator, Uptime) from the main reading area.
- Metric values use `Space Grotesk` or `JetBrains Mono` at large scale, stacked vertically above a micro-label (`label-sm`).

### Status Badges & Diagnostic Chips
- Never rounded into pills; fixed at `2px` or `4px` radius.
- Structural layout: Inset background with a 1px border. For example, Operational Warning: background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`, border `1px solid #F59E0B`, text `#F59E0B`.
- Accompanied by a leading 6px square indicator node rather than a soft blurred circle.

### Precision Data Tables
- Flush full-width dividers without vertical cell borders.
- Header row styled in `#0B1117` with all-caps `JetBrains Mono` label at `10px`, tracked at `0.06em`.
- Alternating hover row states using `#16202C`.
- Numeric data columns right-aligned with locked monospaced figures.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Checkboxes:** Exact `16x16px` squares, `2px` radius, `#1E293B` border, checkmark icon in sharp mechanical stroke. Active fill `#E52B25`.
- **Segmented Toggles:** Enclosed steel container (`#0B1117`) containing segmented buttons separated by hairline dividers. Selected tab turns `#1E293B` with a solid `#FFFFFF` label.
